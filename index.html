How to measure (3 steps)
  1. Lay the phone flat along the limb segment, screen up, top of the phone toward the near joint.
  2. Tap Zero in the start position. Move to end range and read the angle.
  3. Tap Hold to freeze the number and show your partner.

Reads the tilt of the phone from its zeroed position, in whole degrees. Works on any long bone. Interpretation is on the slides.

Works offline after loading. No data leaves your phone. Requires motion sensors (phones and tablets). iPhone: allow motion access when asked.
