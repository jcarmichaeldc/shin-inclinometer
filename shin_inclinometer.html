How to measure (3 steps)
  1. Partner seated, hip and knee at 90°. Rotate the hip inward — the foot swings outward.
  2. Lay the phone flat along the shin, screen up. Read the angle from vertical shin start: tap Zero with the shin vertical, then measure at end range.
  3. Tap Hold to freeze the number and show your partner. Enter the band in the poll.

Bands: under 20° · 20–30° · 30–40° · over 40°. The screen color is the band.

Works offline after loading. No data leaves your phone. Requires motion sensors (phones and tablets). iPhone: allow motion access when asked.
